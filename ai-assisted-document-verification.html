The Challenge

The Challenge

University applications for non-degree courses require students to submit personal and qualification details along with supporting documents such as IELTS/GRE scorecards, passports, national identity documents and academic certificates.

The existing verification process required staff to manually compare every field in the application against the submitted documents. With a high volume of applications, this made verification time-consuming and repetitive, while increasing the possibility of human oversight.

The challenge was to design a verification experience that could automatically identify missing, inconsistent or mismatched information while keeping human reviewers in control of the final decision.

The Solution

The Solution

I designed an AI-assisted document verification workflow that combines OCR, LLM-based data matching and human validation.

OCR extracts relevant information from uploaded documents such as names, dates, identification numbers, qualifications and test scores.

LLM compares the extracted information against the data submitted in the application form.

The system automatically flags missing documents, missing fields or mismatched information for review.

Applications where the data appears consistent are marked as AI-verified, while discrepancies are flagged for human attention.

A human verifier reviews the AI findings and compares the application data with the original documents before making the final verification decision.

If both the AI verification and human verification identify a failure, the application is ultimately marked as Failed.
