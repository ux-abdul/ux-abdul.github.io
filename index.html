Point of view

Three things every AI product designer should keep in mind

AI features earn their place when people understand them, can steer them, and can recover when they’re wrong. These are the three things I keep coming back to.

01

Earn trust, don’t assume it

Models are sometimes wrong, so show your work. Sources, confidence and reasoning let people verify an answer before they act on it.

02

Keep people in control

Let AI take the repetitive, high-volume work, but keep people in charge of consequential decisions, with clear ways to review, correct and override.

03

Design for when AI is wrong

AI will get things wrong. Flag uncertainty, make mismatches obvious, and make errors easy to correct and recover from.
